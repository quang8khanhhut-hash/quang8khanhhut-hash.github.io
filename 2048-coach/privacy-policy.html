2048 Coach — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026. Developer: Big Hedgehog. Contact: quang8khanhhut@gmail.com.

2048 Coach ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information the app keeps on your device

Your games in progress, best scores, statistics, daily challenge results and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. The AI coach that gives hints and reviews your games runs entirely on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information from your device, including the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interactions with ads, and may share it with Google and its advertising partners. This is described in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Google settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or other personal content.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It contains no content unsuitable for young players, but ads are provided by AdMob as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
